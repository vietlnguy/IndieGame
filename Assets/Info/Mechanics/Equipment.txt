SPECIAL EFFECTS TRIGGERS:

During Attacking Combat Sequence:
    - Determine Who Initiated Attack
        - OnAttacking()
    - Target Type Determination
        - OnMeleeTarget()
        - OnRangedTarget()
        - OnSupportTarget()
    - Attacker Type Determination
        - OnRangedAttack()
        - OnMeleeAttack()
    - AttackMove Type Determination
        - OnMagicAttack()
        - OnPhysicalAttack()
    - AttackMove Mana Cost
        - OnManaSpent()
    - Is attacker buffed?
        - OnBuffedDuringAttack()
    - Is target debuffed?
        - OnTargetDebuffed()
    
    - Roll Attack
        - OnHit()
        - OnMiss()
    - Roll Crit
        - OnCrit()
    - What type damage dealt?
        - OnMagicDamageDealt()
        - OnPhysicalDamageDealt()
    - Does attack have chance to debuff?
        - OnAttemptDebuff()
        - OnSuccessfulAttemptDebuff()
        - OnFailedAttemptDebuff()
    
During Defending Combat Sequnce Triggers:
    - Initiated combat
        - OnDefending()
    - What type of attack is being defended?
        - OnDefendingRangedAttack()
        - OnDefendingMeleeAttack()
        - OnDefendingPhysicalAttack()
        - OnDefendingMagicAttack()
    - Does attack miss?
        - OnDodged()
    - What type of damage taken?
        - OnMagicDamageTaken()
        - OnPhysicalDamageTaken()
        - OnCombatDamageTaken()
    - Is there chance to be debuffed?
        - OnDefendingAttemptDebuff()
        - OnDefendingDebuffApplied()
    
End of Turn damage:
    - Trigger OnHpChanged()

When Equipment is equipped:
    - OnMeleeType()
    - OnRangedType()
    - OnSupportType()

Attack Selection Calculation
- Get player stats
    - Copy base stat
    - Copy base modifier
    - Copy base multiplier
- Factor in any pre-battle special effect triggers
    - Attacker
        - OnAttacking() i.e. "+10% base ATK/DEF if initiating combat",
        - OnRangedTarget() i.e. "+50% base ATK/INT if attacking ranged unit.
        - OnMeleeAttack() i.e "+0.25 Mult if initiated melee attack.
        - OnPhysicalAttack() i.e. "+0.5 attackMove multiplier if attackMove deals physical damage"
        - OnManaCost() i.e. "If attackMove costs mana, double it, but increase power (power of heal or damage)
        - OnBuffedDuringAttack() i.e. "Double the strength of buffs. 2X increase from buff
        - OnDebuffedDuringAttack() i.e. "Reduce the strength of negative debuffs"
    - Defender
        - OnDefending()
        - OnDefendingRangedAttack()
        - OnDefendingMeleeAttack()
        - OnDefendingPhysicalAttack()
        - OnDefendingMagicAttack()

- Factor in any buffs/debuffs
    - Apply buffs/debuffs to totalStat, i.e. Dazed reduces totalATK by .67


- Populate UI with calculated stats

            - OnMiss() i.e. "Refund attackMove mana cost", "Gain Charged"
